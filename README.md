# Excel PivotTable Basics

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Excel PivotTables to analyze Superstore sales data. It provides quick summaries of sales by category, region, and month.

## Objectives
- Learn Excel PivotTables.
- Summarize sales data.
- Analyze category-wise sales.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Understand monthly sales trends.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Sample Superstore Dataset

## Project Tasks

### 1. Sales by Category
Analyzed total sales for:
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

### 2. Sales by Region
Analyzed sales across:
- Central
- East
- South
- West

### 3. Monthly Sales
Grouped order dates by year and month to analyze monthly sales trends.

## Key Insights
- Technology has the highest sales among the three categories.
- West is the highest-performing region.
- South has the lowest regional sales.
- Monthly sales vary across different years.
